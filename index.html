What we do

Birch Learning is a tutoring enquiry pilot for a small, anonymized tutoring service. We match learners with the right kind of support — one-to-one, small group, or short exam-prep blocks — and we keep the first step deliberately simple: a short enquiry, a prompt human reply, and a proposed plan.

Who it's for

  • School-age learners (primary through sixth form / college) who need subject support or confidence building.
  • Parents and guardians looking for transparent options and a clear idea of what happens after they get in touch.
  • Adult learners returning to study or preparing for qualifications.

How it works

  1. Send a short enquiry — level, subject, goals, availability, and how you'd like us to reply. That's all we ask for up front.
  2. We reply with a proposed match — which tier fits, what it includes, and indicative pricing, so you can decide with the facts in hand.
  3. You choose the next step — a no-obligation intro session or a straightforward start date.

Options and tiers

1:1 Weekly Tutoring

A dedicated tutor, a consistent weekly slot, and a plan built around the learner's goals. Best for sustained progress.

Small-Group Sessions

Groups of 3–5 learners at a similar level. Collaborative, affordable, and great for motivation.

Exam-Prep Intensives

Short, fixed blocks in the run-up to exams. Focused revision, technique, and practice under realistic conditions.
