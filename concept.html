The current journey, as the evidence shows it

We reviewed how comparable tutoring services handle enquiries and how prospective families experience them. Three patterns stood out:

  • Findability varies wildly. Some services put an enquiry or booking link in the main navigation; others bury it several clicks deep. Families told us they sometimes couldn't even tell whether a service covered their child's level from the homepage.
  • Request burden is often heavy and opaque. Long forms that demand many details — sometimes even payment information — before showing any pricing cause people to abandon the process.
  • Next steps are unclear. Many journeys end at a generic 'thanks' with no indication of what happens next or when, leaving people unsure their enquiry was even received.

What works — and we kept

  • A visible, persistent enquiry entry point in the main navigation.
  • Short forms that ask only for what's needed to respond usefully.
  • Stating tiers and what's included before asking for commitment.

What needs improvement — and what we changed

  • Buried entry points → our homepage and nav link straight to the enquiry form from every page.
  • Over-long, opaque forms → our prototype asks only five essentials (level, subject, goals, availability, reply preference) and never asks for payment details.
  • Silent endings → the form and this site state plainly what happens after you submit: a human reply with a proposed match and indicative pricing.

How the improved concept helps

For learners and families: less effort, no surprises, and a clear promise about what happens next. For the service: fewer abandoned enquiries and better-quality first conversations, because the essentials are captured up front. The concept page and the homepage tell the same story on purpose — what you read here is exactly what the enquiry experience delivers.
